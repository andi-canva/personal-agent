# Learnings Log

Distilled weekly insights — patterns, lessons, and principles that compound over time. Each entry is a condensed summary from `/weekly-wrap`, not raw notes.

The goal: future sessions can reference this file to build on past knowledge without context bloat.

---

## Example Format

### Week of 2026-02-24

**Theme: [What this week was about]**

- **[Insight]:** What you learned and why it matters going forward.
- **[Pattern]:** A recurring observation worth codifying as a principle.
- **[Decision]:** A choice made this week and the reasoning behind it.

---

*Updated automatically by `/weekly-wrap`. You can also add entries manually by saying "remember that..."*
